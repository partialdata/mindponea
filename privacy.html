Mindponea keeps your wellness information on your devices and in Apple Health. We do not transmit it to our servers, sell it, use it for advertising, or track you.

Policy details

Our commitment to your privacy.

We do not receive your personal or health data. The details below explain what Mindponea stores locally and how optional Apple services are used.

1) Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Mindponea handles information when you use the iPhone app and Apple Watch companion. Mindponea is designed to work without an account or a developer-operated server.

2) Data Stored on Your Devices

Mindponea stores session history, session duration, selected techniques, streaks, intentions, mood check-ins, journal entries, reminder preferences, and app settings locally on your device. When you use the Apple Watch companion, the app may also store a session identifier and before-and-after heart-rate summaries locally on your iPhone.

This information is not transmitted to us or stored on servers that we control. The database containing session history, journal entries, and heart-rate summaries is excluded from device backups, so this history will not be restored from a backup.

3) Apple Health and HealthKit

If you grant permission, Mindponea uses HealthKit to read recent heart-rate samples so it can show how your heart rate changed around a session. It may write Mindful Minutes from completed sessions. The Apple Watch companion may start and save a Mind & Body workout in Apple Health to collect heart-rate samples during the session. Ending a session early discards its unfinished Watch workout.

Health information is used only to provide these features. It is not sent to us, shared with third parties, used for advertising or marketing, or sold. Mindponea does not upload its local health information to iCloud. Apple Health manages its own storage and synchronization separately, according to your Apple settings.

4) iPhone and Apple Watch Communication

Mindponea uses Apple's WatchConnectivity framework to exchange session commands, permission status, and heart-rate summaries between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch. This communication occurs through Apple's device services and is not routed through a Mindponea server.

5) Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Mindponea schedules local reminders on your device at the times you choose. Reminder preferences remain on your device, and Mindponea does not use a remote notification service to send these reminders.

6) Analytics, Tracking, and Advertising

The Mindponea app does not include analytics services, advertising networks, or tracking technologies. We do not create user accounts, collect device identifiers, sell personal data, or use health information for advertising or data mining.

7) Retention and Deletion

Locally stored Mindponea data remains on your device until you delete it through an available in-app action or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. Data written to Apple Health, including Mindful Minutes and workouts, is managed separately by Apple and can be reviewed or deleted in the Health app.

8) Your Choices and Permissions

Health permissions are optional. You can review or revoke Mindponea's Health access in the Health app under Sharing > Apps & Services > Mindponea. You can change notification permission in the iPhone Settings app. Revoking permission stops future access but does not automatically delete information already saved in Apple Health.

9) Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else because the app does not transmit personal information to us.

10) Changes to This Policy

If Mindponea's data practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy, revise the effective date above, and update the App Store privacy information when required.

11) Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Mindponea's data practices, contact ryan.summey@gmail.com.
